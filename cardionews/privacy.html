Privacy Policy

CardioNews · Last updated 6 November 2026

CardioNews (“the app”) is developed by Issameddine Ajmi (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information I collect

I do not collect, store or receive any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. I do not run a server for the app, and nothing you do in the app is sent to me.

Information stored on your device

The app saves on your iPhone only: the articles, congress list and training list (Fortbildungen) it has downloaded, so they open quickly and work offline; the articles you save; which articles you have already opened (for the “New” mark); and your alert settings (the topics and keywords you choose). This data stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. Your keywords are never sent anywhere.

Third-party services

To show articles and congresses, the app connects directly from your iPhone to servers that I do not control:

  • OpenAlex (openalex.org), an open index of scientific publications.
  • The news and journal feeds of TCTMD, MedPage Today, ScienceDaily, JACC, Circulation, NEJM and The Lancet.
  • GitHub, which hosts the lists of congresses and Fortbildungen that the app downloads.

When the app connects to these servers, they can see your IP address and technical details such as your device type, as with any website. Their own privacy policies apply. I do not receive this information. When you open an article, it is shown in a Safari view of the original publisher’s website, which has its own privacy policy.

Translation

Translations are made on your iPhone by the translation function of iOS. The text is not sent to me. When you use a language for the first time, iOS may download a language pack from Apple.

Notifications

Alerts are created on your iPhone from the articles it has downloaded. The app does not use a push server and does not send a device token to me. You can turn alerts off in the app or in the iPhone settings under Notifications.

Medical information

The content in the app is not medical advice. See the notes on the support page.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
