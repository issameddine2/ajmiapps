PediaNews

Pediatrics news from around the world

About the app

PediaNews brings new pediatrics papers and news from around the world into one English feed. Titles and summaries in other languages, for example Chinese, Japanese or Korean, are translated automatically on your iPhone, and every item links to the original source. You can save articles, get alerts for topics or keywords you choose, and browse major pediatrics congresses worldwide by topic (such as neonatology, infectious diseases and neurodevelopment) and region. For physicians in Germany there is also a Fortbildungen section with congresses, courses and webinars, starting with the German Society of Paediatrics and Adolescent Medicine (DGKJ).

Requires iOS 18 or later.

Important notes

PediaNews is an information service for professionals and interested readers. It does not give medical advice and must not be used to make decisions about the diagnosis or treatment of individual patients.

Translations are made by machine and can contain mistakes. The original title is shown below each translation, and each item links to the original article. Always check the original before you rely on a statement.

Articles belong to their publishers and authors. PediaNews shows headlines and short excerpts with a link and does not host full texts.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the articles come from?

From two kinds of sources. First, OpenAlex, an open index of scientific publications, filtered to pediatrics, perinatology and child health and to the last 14 days, searched separately for China, Japan, Korea, the rest of Asia, Europe, the Americas, the Middle East and Africa, and Oceania so that no single country dominates. Second, news and journal feeds from MedPage Today, Healio, ScienceDaily, Archives of Disease in Childhood, Pediatric Research, The Lancet Child & Adolescent Health, NEJM and The Lancet. The Sources tab in the app lists them.

Why are some papers missing?

Chinese, Japanese and Korean journals appear only as far as they are indexed in OpenAlex. Some papers have no abstract there and then show only author and journal.

How does the translation work?

Non-English titles and summaries are translated by the translation function built into iOS, on your device. The first time a language appears, iOS asks once to download that language pack.

How often is the feed updated?

When you open the app, and when you return to it if the data is older than 30 minutes. Articles are kept on your iPhone so they are also available offline.

How do alerts work?

In the Alerts tab you choose topics or keywords, for example RSV or asthma. Your iPhone checks for news in the background and shows a notification when something matches. This happens on your device, with no account and no server. iOS decides when the app may check, usually a few times a day, so alerts are not instant. Alerts cover English-language items. You can switch them off in the Alerts tab or in iOS Settings under Notifications.

Where does the congress list come from?

It is compiled from the official pages of the organisers by an automated process that uses an AI model, checked by fixed rules (official web address, plausible dates, fixed topic and region lists), and updated regularly. Errors are possible and dates can change. Please always confirm dates on the organiser's website, which is linked in each entry.

What are the Fortbildungen?

A list of continuing-education offers in Germany, starting with the German Society of Paediatrics and Adolescent Medicine (DGKJ): congresses, Akademie courses, webinars and on-demand courses. Each entry links to the organiser. Programme, fees and CME points (Fortbildungspunkte) are decided by the organiser and the Ärztekammer, so the app does not state them; please check the linked page before booking. The list is compiled and checked in the same way as the congress list and can contain errors.

What does the blue “New” mark mean?

It marks articles you have not opened yet. Use the check button at the top of the News tab to mark everything as read. The mark is stored only on your iPhone.
